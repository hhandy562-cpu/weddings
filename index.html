Common questions

We already get enquiries through Hitched and Bridebook. Does this work with those?

Yes. RevUp responds to enquiries from any source: your own website form, Hitched, Bridebook, or anywhere else. It doesn't matter where the lead comes from. If an enquiry hits your inbox, RevUp can pick it up and reply within seconds.

Do I need a developer or technical person to set this up?

No. Setup is handled for you as part of onboarding. You don't touch any code. By the end of it, enquiries are being replied to and booked automatically. You just check your calendar.

What if a couple wants to call or email instead of booking online?

RevUp handles the first reply and offers a booking slot. If a couple would rather call or continue by email, nothing stops them. They simply don't tap the booking link. RevUp doesn't replace how you communicate. It just makes sure no enquiry goes cold while you're unavailable.

What happens if an enquiry comes in for a date we're already full?

RevUp only offers times and dates you actually have available. If a requested date is gone, the reply acknowledges the enquiry and offers alternatives from your real availability. No double bookings, no awkward follow up.

Will the reply sound like it came from us, or like a bot?

It's written in your venue's voice, with your name and your available dates. To a couple receiving it, it reads like someone on your team was sitting there waiting. There are no generic chatbot phrases or obvious automation tells.

What does this actually cost?

Pricing is set per venue based on enquiry volume and setup. The live test above shows you exactly what a couple experiences. If it works for your venue, we'll talk numbers on a short call. No commitment needed to test it.

What if it doesn't work the way we need it to?

The live test on this page uses real infrastructure, not a simulation. Before anything is agreed, you see exactly how it performs on your actual enquiries. If it doesn't fit, there's nothing to cancel.
